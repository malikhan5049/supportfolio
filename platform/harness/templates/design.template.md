## Intent and Why

Explain the design decision being made and the rationale behind it. Connect it back to the problem the spec defined.

## Goals

List the design-level outcomes this document must achieve. These should translate the spec goals into architectural intent.

## Non-Goals

State design concerns that are explicitly out of scope. Clarify what this design does not attempt to solve.

## Anti-Goals

Describe design outcomes or patterns that must be avoided. Use this to steer reviewers away from tempting but wrong solutions.

## Constraints

Record the non-negotiable limits shaping the design, such as platform choices, security posture, operational budgets, or compatibility requirements.

## Decided Architecture

Present the chosen structure, components, data flow, and interfaces. Explain what was decided and the key trade-offs accepted.

## What's Left to the Builder

Call out decisions intentionally deferred to implementation, along with any guidance or guardrails the builder must follow.
